# Research and execution contract

All dates are exchange session labels. Exchange calendars provide timezone-aware UTC open/close timestamps; the UI displays Australia/Melbourne with IANA DST. NYSE sessions stand in for ordinary consolidated US equities; unusual venue halts and instrument-specific sessions are not known. EOD data cannot recover intrabar ordering, auction queues or depth.

Indicators are causal: rolling SMA20/50/200, EMA20/50/200, seeded Wilder RSI14/ATR14, MACD12/26/9, prior-20 volume ratio, volume percentage change, annualized 20-session close volatility, 63-session relative price strength, prior-window highs and consolidation range. Benchmarks align on the same date. No future interpolation occurs. Splits rebase earlier prices/volume only from their effective session onward when calculating a new row. Vendor split-adjusted volume is reconstructed to as-traded volume using disclosed split events, then rebased to the current segment for indicators. Historical originals/availability cannot be verified from revised vendor downloads.

Rule score: trend = fraction of close>SMA20>SMA50>SMA200 comparisons; momentum = mean of RSI50–72 and positive MACD-histogram conditions; volume = clipped `(relative_volume−0.5)/1.5`; strength = clipped `(63-session excess return+0.1)/0.2`, missing strength scores zero; structure = min(1, detected setup count/2). Each normalized component is multiplied by its displayed configured weight; weights sum to 100. News does not affect ranking. Ties sort by symbol. All six setup definitions are shown in Settings; setups are candidates, never verified signals of profitability.

Entry = signal high + max(0.1% of close, source-currency 0.01); initial stop = entry−2 ATR by default (floored positive); target = entry+2×initial risk by default. Source tick/lot rules are simplified to whole entry shares and a 0.01 minimum trigger increment. ASX initial minimum marketable parcel and venue-specific tick ladders are not modelled. Signals are hypothetical buy-stops valid for three eligible sessions by default.

Only later sessions may fill a historical order. Forward sessions additionally require exchange open **strictly later** than immutable signal recording time; retrieved evidence must already exist at registration. Stale data is excluded from forward scans; historical replays never register as forward tests. A full observed bar is evaluated after it has closed. Missing FX causes an auditable failure and halts further evaluation until repaired, rather than inventing a conversion.

Entry gaps fill at the greater of trigger and opening price. Entry spread is half the configured quoted spread; slippage is adverse on each side. Brokerage is max(minimum AUD fee, notional×commission bps), plus currency-conversion fee on each USD side. Prior-date AUD per USD FX is mandatory (up to seven calendar days old), so a session fill cannot use that session's later FX close. For example USD100 ×10 at FX1.5, spread20bps, slippage10bps, commission10bps/minAUD3 and FX30bps produces entry price100.2, notionalAUD1503, commissionAUD3, conversionAUD4.509, total debitAUD1510.509. Spread/slippage are already in fill price; reported components must not be charged twice.

Sizing respects cash, portfolio risk, maximum position allocation and maximum positions at fill time; pending orders do not reserve cash. Daily entry participation defaults to 1% of reconstructed raw volume. A partial fill cancels the remaining entry quantity IOC. No hidden later quantity fills occur. Protective exits assume the full position can exit with configured friction; this optimistic depth assumption is disclosed. When both target and stop occur within a bar, stop wins. Entry-bar targets are deferred; entry-bar stops are charged conservatively even if true intrabar ordering is unknowable. Stop gaps execute at adverse open, and target gaps get no favorable improvement.

Split events rebase executable pending orders and positions in append-only audit events without altering original signals. Fractional split shares remain synthetic. Dividends use as-traded cash amounts with ex-date share entitlement; provided payment date is used, otherwise ex-date payment is an explicit approximation. AUD entitlement locks at ex-date FX; foreign dividend currency mismatch fails rather than guessing. No tax, franking, withholding, interest, borrow or shorting. Missing payment sessions accrue on the next observed session. Cash dividends outside the selected data horizon may remain receivable.

Backtest and benchmark inputs are frozen. Market ETF and same-stock buy-and-hold use the same source-price, cost, FX and corporate-action model; the benchmark invests available capital rather than matching active exposure. No final liquidation is forced, so terminal marked holdings do not include future exit costs. Historical disappearance is not automatically settled at zero or the last close; missing sessions stop the run. Membership dates, delistings, correction vintages, suspensions, ticker reuse and cash mergers require richer data. The default universe is a small current selection, not a survivorship-free market population.

Forward account assumptions are frozen at creation. Rule edits affect research snapshots and subsequent backtests only. Cancelling a pending order appends an event; there is no API to edit original signal prices or stop levels. SQLite triggers guard audit records and a SHA256 chain detects accidental event/signal corruption. The database owner can bypass application controls, so hashes without external anchoring do not provide tamper-proof assurance.

Returns include all equity, realised and unrealised. Trade statistics use closed trades; expired/rejected orders are failures, not winning or losing trades. Average R uses net AUD trade P/L divided by initial raw price risk in AUD; actual cost/gap losses can exceed −1R. Sharpe uses arithmetic daily returns, 252 sessions/year, zero risk-free rate and at least 30 observations; mixed-market sampling is an approximation. Annualized return is null under 365 days; profit factor is null with no losses. A win-rate Wilson interval is descriptive and assumes independent Bernoulli trials; correlated/selected signals violate that assumption. Neither 100 signals nor a favorable backtest establishes significance or profitability.

Yahoo source contract: quote/history data are split adjusted by the source. The adapter reconstructs as-traded OHLCV and dividends using all effective splits through retrieval, retaining original source observations and factors. Conflicting same-session snapshots require a unique official-session-open daily record; appended quote volumes are never summed. FX is the reciprocal of Yahoo AUDUSD=X close and is used only from a prior date. News is Google News RSS pubDate, not an independently verified publisher timestamp. No sentiment is inferred. These public interfaces are for personal research; their availability and correction histories are not certified.
